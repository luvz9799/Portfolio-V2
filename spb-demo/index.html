About Us

About Akshardham Associates

Akshardham Associates is a trusted property advisory that normally deals in rural plots — along with houses, kothis and farmland. We operate from our main office in Kota, with branch offices in Jhalawar and Khanpur. Our Smart Buyer Network is a new initiative we've just launched — to connect buyers and sellers in one transparent system, with no fake listings and no hidden charges.

Our trained team handles every lead with proper follow-up, and supports you end-to-end — from site visits to bank loans and registry — so your property deal stays easy and secure.
