Guide: how the portal works

How the whole system works, step by step. Come back to this page whenever you are unsure.

The whole flow at a glance

1A seller submits a property through the sell form → the listing goes to Pending.

2You press Activate in Stock / Listings → it goes live on the website.

3A buyer sends a requirement from the website → it appears in Requirements (badge: Self / Website).

4Press Match & Send on it → AI suggests matching listings → pick them and send to the buyer on WhatsApp.

Requirements (buyer leads)

  • Self (Website): the buyer filled in the website form. Telecaller: entered by your telecaller.
  • Status: New → Contacted → Matched (listings sent) → Closed. Change it from the dropdown.
  • Match & Send opens a panel with matching active listings, ranked with a short reason.

Match & Send: what the buyer gets on WhatsApp

  • With AiSensy configured, the buyer gets the options automatically from an approved WhatsApp template. Otherwise a green WhatsApp button opens with the number and message ready.
  • Each property in the message has a link: it opens with all photos, the map and the rate, and shows a preview card in WhatsApp.

Stock / Listings

  • Activate puts a pending listing live. Mark Sold takes it off the website. Re-activate puts it back.
  • Edit changes the name, rate, size, photo visibility and details.
  • Delete removes the listing and its match records for good, after a confirmation.

Other sections

  • Overview & Analytics: listings, requirements and where they come from (charts).
  • Match History: which listing was sent to which buyer.
  • Field Feedback: why visited customers did not register, ranked by reason.
  • Add Property (Agent/Realtor): add a listing directly.
  • Telecaller Panel: a separate page for telecallers to log buyer leads.
